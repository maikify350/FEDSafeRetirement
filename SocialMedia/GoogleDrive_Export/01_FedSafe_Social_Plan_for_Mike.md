## 1. Executive Overview & Value Proposition

FedSafe Retirement is scaling its digital footprint across **Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube**. The mission is to provide specialized, plain-English retirement education to federal and postal employees nearing retirement (within 1 to 5 years).

### Core Brand Positioning
> **"No Generalists. No Side Practices. 100% Focused on Federal & Postal Benefits."**

Unlike general financial planners, FedSafe specializes exclusively in:
- **FERS & CSRS Pension Calculations** & High-3 Optimization
- **Thrift Savings Plan (TSP)** Retirement Income Strategies & Rollovers
- **FEGLI Option B Age-65 Cost Spike Prevention**
- **Postal Service Health Benefits (PSHB)** & Medicare Part B Coordination
- **FERS Special Annuity Supplement** at Minimum Retirement Age (MRA)
- **Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP)** Elections
