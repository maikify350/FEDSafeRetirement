# FedSafe Retirement: Social Media Strategy & Implementation Plan
## Social and Professional Presence (SS_Presence)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

FedSafe Retirement is establishing a coordinated social media presence across three key platforms to build authority, establish credibility, showcase expertise, and attract federal employees within 3 years of retirement. The strategy emphasizes educational content, partner credibility, and trustworthiness over aggressive sales tactics.

**Core Positioning:** "No Generalists. No Side Practices. Just Federal Retirement Experts."

**Primary Call-to-Action:** Schedule a free, no-obligation federal retirement benefit analysis.

---

## BRAND VOICE & MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

### Tone & Personality
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative** – Partners hold RFC® and FRC℠ credentials; deep specialization matters
- **Educational & Accessible** – Simplify complex federal rules without oversimplifying
- **Conversational & Human** – Partner bios highlight real experience and personalities
- **Solution-Focused** – Emphasize clarity, irreversible decision avoidance, and intentional planning

### Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Clarity Beats Complexity** – Federal retirement systems are confusing; we make them clear
2. **Timing Matters** – Missed elections and deadlines have permanent consequences
3. **Specialization Counts** – Unlike generalist advisors, we live and breathe federal benefits
4. **Partner Credibility** – Meet the experts behind the guidance; learn their stories

### Language & Keywords to Emphasize
- Federal retirement consultant, FERS, CSRS, TSP, FEGLI, FEHB, Medicare coordination
- Irreversible elections, benefit coordination, retirement readiness
- "One shot at federal retirement," "Avoid costly mistakes," "Intentional planning"

## CRISIS & BRAND SAFETY

### Moderation Guidelines
- Remove spam and irrelevant comments immediately
- Respond professionally to criticism; offer to take sensitive discussions offline
- Do not engage in political debates (unless directly relevant to federal benefits)
- Flag negative reviews and respond with empathy + solution

### Content to Avoid
- Controversial politics or ideology
- Medical/tax advice (stay general; recommend professional consultation)
- Competitor attacks (focus on OWN value, not tearing down others)
- Hype or "guaranteed returns" claims
