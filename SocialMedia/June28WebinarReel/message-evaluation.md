# FedSafe June 28 Webinar Reel Message Evaluation

## Quick Read

The core message is strong: high stakes, clear audience, urgent event, and a direct call to action. The best version should keep the warning, but tighten the wording so it feels authoritative rather than fear-heavy.

## What Works

- The audience is immediately clear: federal and postal employees nearing retirement.
- The stakes are concrete: one wrong decision can cost tens of thousands.
- FEGLI, survivor benefits, healthcare, pension, and TSP are the right specificity points.
- The specialist positioning is useful: "this is all we do" is stronger than a broad financial planning claim.
- The consultation offer gives people a reason to act now.

## What I Would Tighten

- Change "on line" to "online."
- Change "what are the key decisions" to "the key decisions."
- Change "feel fee" to "feel free."
- Change "love one" to "loved one."
- Avoid saying "this is an event you don't want to miss" because it is generic. Lead with the specific retirement risk instead.
- Keep the phone CTA simple and repeat it visually instead of cramming too much into the voiceover.

## Recommended Voiceover Script If You Re-record

Federal and postal employees nearing retirement: listen carefully.

On Sunday, June 28, FedSafe Retirement is hosting a free online federal retirement webinar.

Here is the truth: you usually get one shot at federal retirement. One wrong decision can cost tens of thousands of dollars.

Many employees do not realize they made a costly election until it is too late to fix. FEGLI, survivor benefits, healthcare eligibility, pension timing, TSP decisions, and retirement income can all affect your long-term security.

At FedSafe Retirement, federal and postal benefits are all we do. We are not generalists. We specialize in helping federal and postal employees understand the decisions that matter before they retire.

In this focused session, you will learn the key choices to review before you finalize your retirement plan.
