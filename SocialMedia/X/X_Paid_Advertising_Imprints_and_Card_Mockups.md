## 1. How X (Twitter) Paid Ads Work for Federal Retirement

X Ads allow you to run **Promoted Website Cards** and **Promoted Video Cards** that appear seamlessly in the home timelines of people who follow `@USOPM`, `@GovExec`, `@TSP4gov`, `@FederalTimes`, and `@USPS`.

### Core Ad Formats on X:
1. **Website Card Ad (Image + Clickable Destination):**
   - Image Ratio: 1.91:1 (800 x 418 px or 1200 x 628 px) or 1:1 Square (1080 x 1080 px).
   - Tweet Text: Up to 280 characters (clean, bold question).
   - Website Title / Callout: Up to 70 characters (e.g., *"Schedule Your Free Federal Benefit Review"*).
2. **Promoted Video Card:**
   - 16:9 Landscape or 9:16 Vertical video with native click-through card.
   - Autoplays in timeline with sound on tap.
