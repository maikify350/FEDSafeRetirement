## 3. Tone & Editorial Guardrails

### ✅ What We Always Do:
- **Education-First:** Frame content around checklists, risk prevention, and translating complex OPM/USPS acronyms into plain English.
- **Calm & Objective Guidance:** Help employees slow down, verify numbers, and avoid rushed retirement decisions.
- **Partner Authority:** Highlight partner credentials (RFC®, FRC℠, Veteran-Owned, 20+ years in federal benefits).
- **Agency Vendor Ready:** Clearly state FedSafe is available for bid to partner with federal agencies, contracting officers, and HR coordinators for workforce training.

### ❌ Strict Prohibitions (Never Violate):
- ❌ **NO high-pressure sales talk, hype, or fear-mongering.**
- ❌ **NO specific financial, tax, or investment return guarantees.**
- ❌ **NO political, partisan, or agency-attack content.**
- ❌ **NO generic advice — always tailored to federal systems (FERS, CSRS, TSP, FEGLI, FEHB, PSHB, ORA).**
