# FedSafe Partner Seminar Image Prompts

These prompts are tailored to the four partner headshots in `Partners\` and the FedSafe branding assets in `logos\`.

Global constraints for every seminar image:

- Preserve the likeness of the referenced partner headshot.
- Dress the presenter in a business-casual shirt, quarter-zip, or polo with a visible embroidered FedSafe Retirement logo on the chest.
- Do not use a suit jacket, tie, or formal bio-photo wardrobe.
- Avoid the repeated straight-on podium pose used in earlier seminar generations.
- Include a realistic audience with more middle-aged women than before.
- Make the audience look engaged, thoughtful, and professional, not like stock-photo extras.
- Use a premium photorealistic editorial style suitable for client-facing marketing review.
- Avoid distorted text, misshapen hands, duplicated faces, identical audience members, or exact copies of prior seminar compositions.
